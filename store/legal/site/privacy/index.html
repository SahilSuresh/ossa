Privacy Policy

Effective 26 October 2026

The short version. Your training, check-ins, body data, health data and photos stay on your phone. We don't run ads, we don't use analytics, we don't sell data, and we don't track you across other apps or websites. Only three things leave your phone: what's needed to run your subscription, crash reports so we can fix bugs, and, if you turn on the weather, a rough location for the forecast.

Who we are

Ossa ("the app") is made by Andrej Gazi, an independent developer based in the United Kingdom. Andrej Gazi is the data controller for the personal data described here. You can contact us at andrejgazi25@gmail.com.

Data that stays on your phone

Ossa does not need an account. The following is stored only on your device and is never sent to us:

  • Your profile answers (such as your name, goal, age range, training days and equipment)
  • Your workouts, sets, weights, check-ins, sore spots you flag, focus sessions and your levels and ranks
  • Health data you choose to share from Apple Health or Health Connect: steps, sleep and resting heart rate
  • Posture scan photos. They are analysed on your phone (by Google's MediaPipe model, which runs on the device) and are never uploaded
  • Reminder times and the data shown in Ossa's widgets

Deleting the app deletes this data. If you use your phone's own backup (such as iCloud backup), that backup is handled by Apple or Google under their privacy terms, not by us.

Apple Health and Health Connect

Ossa only reads health data if you give permission, and only to adjust your Body Score inside the app. Ossa never writes to Apple Health or Health Connect, never sends health data off your device, and never uses it for advertising or shares it with anyone. You can withdraw permission at any time in your phone's settings.

What we don't do

  • No advertising and no advertising SDKs
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No selling or renting of personal data
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites
  • No use of health data or photos for anything outside the app's own features

How long we keep data

Subscription records are kept for as long as you have, or had, a subscription and then as long as needed for tax and accounting rules (normally up to 6 years). Crash reports are kept for up to 90 days. Emails are kept for as long as needed to help you, then deleted. We don't store weather requests; MET Norway's own logs are covered by its privacy policy.

International transfers

RevenueCat and Sentry process data in the United States. Where data leaves the UK or EU, it is protected by appropriate safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum and the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

Your rights

Under UK and EU data protection law you can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, object to or restrict how we use it, and ask for a copy in a portable format. Because Ossa has no accounts, the easiest way to delete your on-device data is to delete the app. To ask us to delete subscription records or crash reports, email us from the Settings screen so we can find your app user ID. We'll reply within one month.

If you're unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk or to your local data protection authority.

Children

Ossa is not intended for children under 13 and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update this page and the date at the top. Significant changes will also be shown in the app.
