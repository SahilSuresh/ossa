## Description (3934/4000)
```
Your body is the character. Train it, and watch it level up.
Ossa shows your own body in 3D with every muscle ranked, from Fragile to Unbreakable.

Ossa needs a subscription (Ossa Pro). There is no free version. The yearly plan starts with a 7-day free trial.

Your body starts as stone. Answer a few questions and it breaks free. Your weakest muscle is marked as your weak link, with the sessions it needs to rank up.

RANK EVERY MUSCLE
Each muscle climbs five ranks: Fragile, Stable, Strong, Forged and Unbreakable. Each rank has three steps and its own colour on your body. Ranks never drop. Leave a muscle alone for ten days and its colour starts to fade, until you train it again.

A LEVEL THAT ONLY GOES UP
Training sets earn rank points for the muscles they work, and the points add up to your level: a hex emblem with a ring that fills towards the next one. Levels unlock the Bones layer, then new finishes for your body.

TODAY'S QUEST
One session a day, built around your training days, your kit (a gym, dumbbells at home or just your body) and what has recovered. You see the muscles it works lit on your body, how long it takes and the rank points it pays. Your points count up as you log your sets. Last time's numbers sit beside each set, with a rest timer and plates per side for barbell lifts. Tap how hard each lift felt and Ossa sets next time's target.

AFTER EVERY WORKOUT
Step through your results: personal bests, the points each muscle earned, rank-ups, level-ups, trophies and your streak. Planned rest days never break the streak.

SIDE QUESTS
Once a day your body dares you to do one thing. It might be a muscle that's starting to fade, a cold finish, more steps or your phone down by ten. Turn on weather dares and the dare can follow the forecast. Pick a kind Coach voice or a tough-love Drill.

RECOVER
See which muscles are ready and when the rest will be back. Log protein and carbs in Fuel. Flag a sore spot on your body and get a short routine for that area. Plus a mobility flow, a 60-second desk reset, a posture scan and an evening wind-down.

FOCUS
Guided breathing, calm sessions and Monk Mode: a 25, 50 or 90-minute focus timer with an optional task. Breathing and Monk Mode grow your aura, the light around your body.

LEARN
Short lessons played out on your own figure: what stress does, why muscles get sore, where fat goes when you lose it.

EVOLVE
Trophies in bronze, silver and gold. Your ideal self: the gap to your next overall rank. Prestige laps for top-rank muscles. Your path, Tank or Scout. A card of your rank to share. And your look: finishes and materials for your body.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account and no ads. What you log stays on your phone. If you connect Apple Health, Ossa reads your steps, sleep and resting heart rate and never writes to Health. The posture scan is read on your phone and never uploaded. Weather dares are off until you turn them on. Then only an approximate location (about 1 km) is shared: with MET Norway for the forecast and with Apple for the town name.

OSSA PRO
Ossa Pro is the whole app: your ranked 3D body and your level, every workout, Recover, Focus, Learn and Evolve. It also adds three things built around you:
- your training plan, built from your answers (four build weeks, then a lighter one)
- fixes in every week, with your weakest body-check and posture results worked in
- three new materials for your whole body (Obsidian, Chrome and Carbon fibre), on iPhones that can draw them
Choose yearly, with a 7-day free trial, or monthly. Payment is charged to your Apple ID when you buy, or when the free trial ends. Ossa Pro renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the period. Manage or cancel any time in your App Store account settings.

Terms of Use (EULA): https://getossa.app/terms/
Privacy Policy: https://getossa.app/privacy/

Ossa is for general fitness and wellbeing. It is not medical advice.
```

- **Keywords:**

  - Added `back,pain` (the two words together cover "back pain"), `training`, `stretching`, `map`, `game`, `home` and `plan`.
- **Promo:** "Train today's quest" is now "Do today's quest."
- **Description, the false claims fixed:**
  - Only training sets earn rank points. Stretches and mobility moves earn none.
  - The weak link no longer claims "today's quest starts there". It now says the weak link is marked, with the sessions it needs to rank up.
- **Description, the rest:**
  - The subscription line is now paragraph two and says "There is no free version".
  - The fade is the colour only. "Your level never drops" is cut, because the heading already says it.
  - The effort tap now sets "next time's target", because bodyweight moves get reps, not weight.
  - Aches: you now flag a sore spot on your body and get a short routine "for that area". "Aching?" opens Sore spots.
  - The aura grows from breathing and Monk Mode only.
  - The weather sentence now also names Apple, which supplies the town name.
  - The trial billing line, "Ossa Pro renews…", and the Terms of Use (EULA) and Privacy Policy labels are fixed.

  - "Ossa 2.0" is now "The redesign", and "aches" is now "sore spots".

  - The session comes from the plan, not "the weakest muscles".
