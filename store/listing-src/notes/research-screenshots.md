**Screenshot research: App Store sets for Ossa (dark, lime accent, 3D body)**

ScreensDesign never returned a conversation_id, so I made every call one at a time. The notes come from its descriptions of each listing creative, and the URLs are at the end.

## The sets studied (11)

**1. Gravl (AI trainer). The closest match to Ossa's palette.**
- Background: textured charcoal on every slide.
- Headlines: white with the key phrase in lime, sentence case, ending in a full stop, 3–6 words. Examples: "Get stronger. Every session." (4), "Progressive overload, automated." (3), "Machine taken? Swap it." (4), "Recover as hard as you train." (6).
- Slide 1: copy aligned left, with two tilted phones in the bottom-right corner.
- Slide 2: two overlapping tilted phones, with see-through stat chips floating over them.
- Slide 3: one large phone.
- Later slides use one phone or a floating UI card. One slide is a photo of the coaches with chat bubbles over it.
- Colours: black, white and lime only.

**2. Symmetry (muscle ranks). The direct competitor.**
- Solid black, one full phone in the centre, a bold white headline at the top, 3–4 words: "Get your rank", "Get a custom plan", "Track your workouts", "Build an aesthetic body".
- Hero: a body map in rank colours, with tier badges and a percentile bar.
- First three slides: rank, then plan, then tracking.
- It's clean but every slide looks the same, and there's no frame-breaking moment.

**3. MacroFactor**
- Solid black, ALL CAPS, 2–4 words: "LOG FOODS FASTER", "MASTER YOUR METABOLISM", "END SCALE ANXIETY".
- The hero is an editorial still with no phone: the wordmark plus "SMART MACRO TRACKER". After that, one centred phone per slide, with the same layout every time.
- Strictly monochrome; the only colour comes from the UI.

**4. Strava**
- Black at the top fading to brand orange or red at the bottom, the same on every slide.
- The phone frame itself is painted brand orange.
- Sentence-case white headlines, 3–6 words: "Track your workouts", "Lift it. Log it. See your gains."
- Slide 1 is a real Apple award plus the logo.
- It uses one hue throughout.

**5. Fitbod**
- Dark throughout, ALL CAPS: "SMART WORKOUTS BUILT FOR YOU" (5).
- Slide 1 has the Editor's Choice badge and a review count.
- Slide 2 is the muscle-recovery body map with a quote and 5 stars.
- Slide 5 drops the phone: a floating UI card with a gold trophy and "New Strength Record!".
- One accent: red.

**6. Liftoff (ranked gym)**
- Dark UI on a light blue or grey canvas, ALL CAPS.
- Hero: "TURN YOUR WORKOUTS INTO A GAME" (6) on the left, plus laurels for member count and rating.
- Slide 2: three overlapping phones.
- Slide 3: "GET YOUR RANK ON OVER 500 EXERCISES" (7) with a Gold III badge. The Bodygraph comes on slide 5.
- Its headlines are wordy compared with Gravl and MacroFactor.

**7. GymStreak**
- Hero: a glowing see-through 3D anatomy figure with no phone, panels floating around it, and "YOUR AI PERSONAL COACH" (4).
- The other slides: a tilted phone on purple, ALL CAPS, 3–5 words ("SCORE XP FOR EVERY SET", "RISE IN GYM RANK"), with a bronze rank emblem shown in the UI.

**8. GymLevels**
- Deep purple with gold runes, serif headlines: "The Gym, Gamified" (3), "Earn XP in Your Workouts".
- Slide 2 lets a purple aura spill out of the phone, a useful frame-breaking trick.
- It's overloaded with ornament, so treat it as a warning as well as a reference.

**9. Flighty (non-fitness benchmark for a premium look)**
- Hero: a 3D-rendered plane over a wireframe map, with no phone and real Apple Design Award badges: "First-class flight tracker. Fly smarter."
- Slide 2: a press quote over a tilted phone.
- Slide 3: Live Activities, shown on two phones.
- Slide 5: a dark photo with translucent UI cards over it.

**10. Gentler Streak (character)**

- Hero: a hand holding the phone, plus a press quote.

**11. Finch, Duolingo and Opal (character and story)**

## 10 rules that make a set look professional in 2026

1. **Slide 1 is a poster, not a screenshot.** The best sets show the app's core object at large size with no phone: GymStreak's 3D figure, Flighty's plane, Muscle & Motion's 3D arm, MacroFactor's still. Pair it with a promise of 5 words or fewer. The phone comes in on slide 2 or 3.
2. **Headlines are 2–6 words with one idea and lead with a verb or a benefit.** Two lines at most. Gravl, Symmetry and MacroFactor average 3–4 words; Liftoff's 7-word lines look weaker.
3. **Pick one type system and never mix.**
   - Either ALL CAPS heavy (MacroFactor, Fitbod, GymStreak) or sentence case with a full stop (Gravl, Strava, Opal).
   - At 1320×2868, use weight 800–900, tracking about −2%, line height about 1.0.
   - Cap height should be at least about 110 px so it still reads in the search-results thumbnail.
4. **Use a two-tone headline: one keyword or phrase in the accent colour.** Gravl does this in lime, Bevel and Muscle & Motion in orange. Never use more than one accent.
5. **Use one background system for the whole strip.**
   - Options: solid black (Symmetry, MacroFactor), black fading to the brand colour (Strava), or one constant gradient (Flighty, Opal).
   - A different colour per slide (Gentler Streak) looks less premium for a dark brand.
6. **Dark UI needs separating from a dark canvas.** Use a thin graphite or brand-coloured frame (Strava), a soft glow or vignette behind the phone, or a contrasting canvas (Liftoff and Ladder put dark UI on light or lime).
7. **Show the phone big.** It should be 75–85% of the canvas width and may run off the bottom edge. Crop low-value UI (tab bars) rather than shrinking the phone.
8. **Lift one element out of the screen.** Take one card (stat chip, trophy, set row) and scale it about 1.2–1.4× over or beside the phone: Hevy's set rows, Fitbod's trophy card, Gravl's chips, GymLevels' aura. This is the "zoomed detail" that makes a slide look designed.
9. **The first three slides tell the story.** Slide 1 is identity and the promise, slide 2 the core loop, slide 3 what sets the app apart:
   - Symmetry: rank, then plan, then track.
   - Gravl: promise, then progress, then automation.
   - Liftoff: game, then overview, then rank.

   Subheads are rare. When used, keep them to 8 words or fewer, at 40–50% of the headline size, in muted grey.
10. **Proof has to be honest, and the set should end deliberately.**
    - Ossa can't use ratings or laurels, so copy Gentler Streak's privacy slide instead: no account, no ads, everything stays on your phone.
    - End on a feature grid (Flighty's "All this.") or a collection slide.
    - Keep the headline position and phone size identical on every slide so the strip reads as one series.

## 3 layout systems for Ossa

**A. "The Body, frameless" (hero; slide 1, plus maybe a "First form" slide)**
- **Canvas and body:** a pure black canvas with the in-app 3D body rendered large (about 70% of the canvas height). The body shows rank colours per muscle, the weak link circled in lime and a faint floor glow.
- **Chips beside the body:** two or three real UI pieces, not a full phone: the hex level emblem with its XP ring ("Level 13"), and the weak-link card. These chips show the app in use, so it stays within Apple's rules.
- **Headline:** at the top, 2 lines, white with one lime keyword.
- **References:** GymStreak hero, Flighty hero, Muscle & Motion. The "Out of the Stone" statue with lime cracks also fits this layout.

**B. "Black canvas, big phone, lifted card" (the workhorse; slides 2–8)**
- **Canvas and phone:** solid #000 with the phone at about 80% of the width in a thin graphite frame. The phone sits under the headline and runs off the bottom, with a soft lime or bone glow behind it.
- **Lifted card:** one UI card pulled out and scaled about 1.3× over the phone's edge, for example:
  - the muscle meter ("Triceps · Forged II")
  - "+3 RP · 4/19 sets"
  - the quest card
  - a gold personal best
- **Headline:** sentence case, 3–5 words, ending in a full stop, keyword in lime.
- **References:** Gravl, Symmetry, MacroFactor, Fitbod's card, Hevy's break-out rows.

**C. "Reward moment, full-bleed" (1–2 slides, for example slide 3)**
- **Layout:** an in-app reward screen fills the canvas edge to edge with no phone frame: a rank-up hex badge with rays ("FORGED II"), Level up, or the "FULL WEEK" trophy.
- **Headline:** sits over the top.
- **Colour:** on that slide only, the tier colour (gold or cyan) is allowed as the second colour, in place of lime.
- **References:** Duolingo's frameless UI, Fitbod's trophy card, the moment GymLevels' aura spills out.
- **Optional extra:** one Ladder-style lime canvas slide with black type, as a pattern break in a dark strip.

**Suggested order:**

| Slide | Layout | Content |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A | Body, level and weak link |
| 2 | B | Today's quest or workout HUD (the loop) |
| 3 | C | Rank-up (the differentiator) |
| 4–8 | B | Run results, Recover, Focus or Monk Mode, Learn, Evolve or Your look |
| 9 | B | Privacy: "No account. No ads." |
| 10 | — | Feature grid |
