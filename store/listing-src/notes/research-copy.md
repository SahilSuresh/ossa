== DESCRIPTION (4000 max) ==
Your body is the character. Train it, and watch it level up.
Ossa shows your own body in 3D with every muscle ranked, from Fragile to Unbreakable.

It starts as stone. Answer a few questions and it breaks free. Your weakest muscle is your weak link, and today's quest starts there.

Ossa needs a subscription (Ossa Pro). The yearly plan starts with a 7-day free trial.

RANK EVERY MUSCLE
Each muscle climbs five ranks, Fragile, Stable, Strong, Forged and Unbreakable, each in three steps, and shows its rank in colour on your body. Ranks never drop. A muscle you leave alone for ten days starts to fade until you train it again.

A LEVEL THAT ONLY GOES UP
Every set earns rank points, and they add up to your level: a hex emblem with a ring that fills towards the next one. Your level never drops, and as it climbs it unlocks more: the Bones layer, then new finishes for your body.

TODAY'S QUEST
One session a day, built around your week, your kit (a gym, dumbbells at home or just your body) and what has recovered. You see the muscles it works lit on your body, how long it takes and the rank points it pays. In the workout, your points count up with every set you log. Last time's numbers sit beside each set, with a rest timer and plates per side for barbell lifts. Tap how hard each exercise felt and Ossa sets next time's weight.

AFTER EVERY WORKOUT
Step through your results: personal bests, the points each muscle earned, rank-ups, level-ups, trophies and your streak. Planned rest days never break the streak.

SIDE QUESTS
Once a day your body dares you to do one thing: a muscle you've been neglecting, a cold finish, more steps, or your phone down by ten. Turn on weather dares and the dare fits the forecast. Pick a kind Coach voice or a tough-love Drill.

RECOVER
See which muscles are ready, which are still recovering and when they'll be back. Log protein and carbs in Fuel. Tell Ossa what aches (neck, back, hips, knees or wrists) and get a short routine for it. A mobility flow, a 60-second desk reset, a posture scan and a wind-down for the evening.

FOCUS
Guided breathing, calm sessions, and Monk Mode: a 25, 50 or 90-minute focus timer with an optional task. Breathing and focus grow your aura, the light around your body.

LEARN
Short lessons played out on your own figure: what stress does, why muscles get sore, where fat goes when you lose it.

EVOLVE
Trophies in bronze, silver and gold. Your ideal self: the gap to your next overall rank. Prestige laps for muscles at the top. Your path, Tank or Scout. A card of your rank to share. And your look: finishes and materials for your whole body.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account and no ads. What you log stays on your phone. If you connect Apple Health, Ossa reads your steps, sleep and resting heart rate and never writes to Health. The posture scan is read on your phone and never uploaded. Weather dares are off until you turn them on, and then send only an approximate location (about 1 km) to MET Norway for the forecast.

OSSA PRO
Ossa is a subscription app with no free version: Ossa Pro is the whole app. It includes your ranked 3D body and your level, every workout, Recover, Focus, Learn and Evolve, and three things built around you: your training plan, built from your answers (four build weeks, then a lighter one); fixes in every week, with your weakest body-check and posture results worked in; and three new materials for your whole body, Obsidian, Chrome and Carbon fibre, on iPhones that can draw them. Yearly with a 7-day free trial, or monthly. Payment is charged to your Apple ID. It renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the period. Manage or cancel any time in your App Store account settings.

Terms: https://getossa.app/terms/
Privacy: https://getossa.app/privacy/

Ossa is for general fitness and wellbeing. It is not medical advice.
