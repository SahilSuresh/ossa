# Ossa UI system: layout, design and onboarding

Rebuilt 2026-10-04, redesigned 2026-10-15 (the "Symmetry" look: near-black, bone buttons, lime, heavy wide headlines, muscle ranks), and given the Ossa 2.0 look on 2026-10-16 (Phase 0 of "The Body as a Character": pure black, neon lime, Inter, glass, hexagons, the brief's rank colours).
Through those three rounds the 3D body, scoring, plans, workouts and the native bridge stayed as they were. What changed was the layer you see and four new features: muscle ranks, a real streak, daily dares and a Recover tab.
Phase 1, "Vessel Engine" (2026-10-16), moved scoring, the workout record, fading and recovery into one engine file, `web/ui/vessel.js`, and added Sore spots. See "Vessel engine" and "Sore spots" below.
Phase 2, "Train" (2026-10-16), added Exercises, Kinetic chain and Where your sets went (with Past sessions), the exercise data behind them, and the Build 9 native batch: bridge v2 (haptics, keep-awake, approximate location, MET Norway weather) and widgets in rank colours. See "Exercises and Kinetic chain", "Where your sets went", "Native bridge v2" and "Widgets" below.
Phase 3, "Focus" (2026-10-17), renamed the third tab Focus and added Aura XP (breathing and focus only), the aura on the body (a still halo on the Body page, stills on Focus), Monk Mode and Heartbeat, and moved the Mind screens off violet onto the tokens. Web only. See "Focus", "Aura XP", "Monk Mode", "Heartbeat" and "Body effects" below.
Phase 4, "Recover" (2026-10-17), made Recover a one-screen home with tiles and added Fuel and the 3-minute reset (see `recover.js` in the file map).
Phase 5, "Evolve" (2026-10-17), made Profile's first segment the Evolve hub and added the Trophy room, Ideal self, Prestige laps, Your path (Tank or Scout), Your card, Your look and Ossa Pro's three materials, with the body looks drawn by our own renderer. Web only. See "Evolve", "Body effects" and "Vessel engine" below.
Phase 6, "Challenge" (2026-10-17), added weather dares: an opt-in that turns the daily life dare into a short weather dare when the forecast allows (or "Stay in" on a rough or dark day), a safety gate proved by `scripts/check-dares.js`, the dare page, the weather dares page with its Settings row, and the Weatherproof trophy. The dares moved out of ranks.js into dares.js. Web only. See "Dares" and "Weather dares" below.

Backups: `C:\Ossa\backup-before-ui-rebuild-2026-10-04\`, `C:\Ossa\backup-before-redesign-2026-10-15\` and `C:\Ossa\backup-before-redesign-v2-2026-10-16\` (web/, scripts/, docs/, native/, ossaHtml.js as they were before each change). From 2.0 on the project is also in git: a baseline commit on `master`, the redesign on the branch `redesign-v2`. To roll back the redesign, copy that folder's `web` and `scripts` back over `ossa-expo`.

## Muscle ranks

- Ranks sit on each muscle's score (0–100), which rank points (RP) raise: see "Vessel engine". The overall number is the "RP average". `RANKS` and `RK_STEPS` in `ossa.html`: Unranked (0), Fragile (1–19), Stable (20–39), Strong (40–59), Forged (60–79), Unbreakable (80–100), each with levels I / II / III. A muscle you keep training ranks up every couple of sessions.
- `rankIx` is the fine step (0–15) so every level-up counts as a rank-up; `rankTier` is 0–5; `rankOf` gives "Stable II"; `toNext` the points to the next level. The muscle sheet, toasts and the Profile's rank count use these.
- Ranks never drop. A muscle left alone for 10 days starts to fade on the 3D body and is fully faded at 21 (`fadeOf`). A flagged muscle doesn't fade.
- **Weak link** (`rkWeak`): the lowest-ranked trained muscle in your focus area from onboarding, else the lowest overall, else the first muscle of your focus area. Flagged muscles are skipped.
- **Muscle ranks screen** (`openRanks`): the "43 RP average" hero. The FRONT and BACK cards are `Body.shot` stills in rank colours with no red (`rkShots`, cached in `rkShots.c` by figure, finish, scores and fade steps). Tap one to open 3D on that side. Without 3D they fall back to `uiRankFig`, where a fading part is drawn at fill-opacity 1 − 0.55 × fade. `rkFadeShown(k)` is 0 for a flagged muscle, the same rule as the 3D body. Row tags: Weak link, Fading, Flagged, and since Phase 5 the provider tags from `rkTagX` (prestige stars, "Prestige ready"). Phase 5 also added an "Ideal self ›" link under the hero.
- **Rank-up** (`rkCelebrate`): reads `TR.result.ranks` in its order (trained muscles in session order, then helpers by gain), shows at most three muscle segments (the third says "And N more muscles ranked up"), then an overall segment last when the overall rank went up. The best set comes from `rkBestSet` (exercises for that muscle first; a 1-rep max only for weighted lifts). `rkGainPer(s)` = max(1, 3.6 × (1 − s/125)), the expected gain a session.

## Streak

`uiStreak()` in `ranks.js`: days in a row you kept your plan. A trained day counts, a planned rest day in between keeps it going, today only counts once you've trained and can't break it. Days before you started never show as missed. `S.streak` is kept as the old count of training days but nothing shows it any more.

## Dares (Andrej's rules)

Once a day. Half the days a lift dare when a muscle qualifies (trained before and fading: `stateOf` is "fading", so 10+ idle days, not flagged, not recovering), never more than about 1.5× the reps you've logged and 80% of your best weight; otherwise a life dare (cold finish, steps, phone down, protein before noon, hip mobility). Coach tone by default, Drill on the card's switch; it roasts the neglect, never the person. "Not today" costs nothing (no streak loss) and shrinks the next dare; "Done" grows it back a little. A lift dare whose muscle gets flagged turns into a life dare, unless it's done or turned down. State in `S.dares`. Since Phase 5 the split comes from `rkDareMix()`: half and half by default, Tank 65% lift dares, Scout 35% with life dares weighted to steps and mobility (it applies from the next day; today's dare stays).

Since Phase 6 the dares live in `web/ui/dares.js` and `dares.css` (moved from ranks.js; `DARE_LIFE` is now `dareLife()`, same ids). With weather dares on, a notable day's **life** dare can become a weather dare; lift dares are untouched (see "Weather dares"). Lift dares use the muscle's first exercise counted in reps, never a carry or a hold, and never more than 1.5× the logged reps (BUG-30). Every dare card now has a solid lime-tinted border, an fs-4 800 title, the lime button first (44 px) and "Not today" as a 44 px link.

## Body effects (Ossa 2.0)

Effects drawn on our own 3D body go through `window.BODY_FX` in `web/ui/body-fx.js`, which is included before the body is built.
- `BODY_FX.add({id, decl, muscle, final, vdecl, vertex, hdecl, hull, uniforms, draw, busy, post})` (`hdecl`, `hull` and `post` since Phase 3, below). `decl` holds uniforms and helpers. `muscle` runs inside the muscle layer after the rank colour, with `id`, `w`, `vR` (+x is the figure's left), `q`, `fib`, `edge`, `bl`, `heat`, `isSel`, `dif`, `col` and `rf` (this muscle's rank-mode fade, 0–1) in scope. `final` runs just before the pixel is written. `draw(gl,U,pick)` sets the effect's uniforms each frame. `busy()` returning true makes the body redraw at about 30 fps, only while it's on screen.
- `vdecl` and `vertex` (Phase 1) go into the vertex shader used by the body, picking and the outline, after skinning and breathing. In scope: `r` (rest position), `nr` (rest normal), `M` (skin matrix), `n` (world normal) and `w` (world position: move it to change the shape). `vR` stays the rest position, so colours and picking follow. With a vertex chunk, `draw` also runs for the outline pass, as `draw(gl,UH,false)`.
- `BODY_FX.inShot` and `BODY_FX.shotFlags` are true only while `Body.shot` draws a still. An effect that shows red skips it when `inShot && !shotFlags`.
- If any effect fails to compile, the body is built without effects (a warning in the console), never the flat 2D figure. When iOS drops the 3D context, the renderer rebuilds everything in place.
- `Body.palette()` re-reads the rank colours from the tokens. `Body.fxRebuild()` rebuilds after an effect is added at run time.
- Phase 1 added to the Body API: `Body.shot({yaw=0, rank=true, flags=false, sel, canvas=false, h=720})` draws a still of the standing body in rank colours with no background and returns a PNG data URL (about 345×720, on black), a canvas, or null (no WebGL, onboarding, a lost context). `Body.setRankFade(on)` turns the rank-mode fade on or off (on by default). `Body.setPose(pose|null)` sets a pose at once with no easing; null goes back to standing. Phones without WebGL get stubs (`shot` returns null).
- Phase 2 added two `Body.shot` options. `mask: {muscleKey: 0..1}` lights those muscles ice-blue on a dark body (rank colours, the selection edge and sore-spot red are off), e.g. `Body.shot({yaw:0, mask:{pec_major:1, triceps:0.45}})`. `crop: [top, bottom]` keeps part of the figure box (default `[0, 1]`; `[0.08, 0.6]` is the upper body, `[0.42, 1]` the lower body) at native resolution, so a crop returns a shorter image. The mask state is saved and restored around the shot, so the live body comes back in rank colours. Kinetic chain and Where your sets went use it, with `heatSVG` as the fallback when it returns null. The Muscle ranks cards (no mask) are the regression check.
- **Phase 3: the outline shader and the glow pass.** `BODY_FX.add` also takes `hdecl` and `hull`, pasted into the outline fragment shader (`HULL_FS`) at `/*FX:hdecl*/` (top level) and `/*FX:hull*/` (in main). In scope: `vW` (world position, after any vertex move), `vN` (world normal), `vR` (rest position), `uRo` (the camera), `uA`, `uHull`, and three locals: `col` (the outline's opaque colour, written when `uHull` is 1), `add` (the glow pass's colour) and `glow` (true in the glow pass). Write `add` only when `glow`; a glow pixel whose `add` stays 0 is dropped. With no effect loaded the outline is exactly as before. The outline program gets the effect code when any of `vdecl`, `vertex`, `hdecl` or `hull` has chunks; if it fails to compile it is built plain.
  - Vertex chunks see `uHull`: 0 for the body and picking, 1 for the outline, 2 for the glow pass. Gate a shape change for the glow on `uHull > 1.5`.
  - **The glow pass**: after the body, only when `!pick && BODY_FX.active && BODY_FX.post()` is true on that frame. The outline mesh is drawn again with `uHull` 2 (pushed out 2 px), back faces only, depth-tested but not written, added with `ONE, ONE`; then depth writes and back-face culling are restored. `draw(gl, UH, false)` runs for the outline and again for the glow pass, so set every uniform both times. It also runs inside `Body.shot`.
  - `BODY_FX.post()` is true when any effect's `post()` is; nothing is drawn while none asks. `BODY_FX.draw` runs each effect in its own `try`, so one that throws can't leave another's uniforms stale (uniforms keep their last value on the program).
  - `BODY_FX.shotFx`: the `fx` object passed to `Body.shot`, set with `inShot` while the still draws, null otherwise. A stills-only effect draws only when `inShot && shotFx && shotFx[id]`.
- **Phase 3 `Body.shot` options** (a shot without them is byte-for-byte as before; ranks.js, discover.js and density.js pass none):
  - `pose`: a pose object with `boneMats` keys (root, pelvis, spine, head, thR/thL, shR/shL, ftR/ftL, uaR/uaL, faR/faL, twR/twL, shrug) drawn instead of standing, for this still only. A pose that throws falls back to standing (console warning). The live pose is never touched; never call `Body.setPose` for a still.
  - `fx`: any object, exposed as `BODY_FX.shotFx` while this still draws.
  - Framed mode, on when `at` is three finite numbers and `span > 0`: `at` [x, y, z] is drawn at the centre and `span` is the metres shown top to bottom. `size` [w, h] in px (default the live canvas bitmap, about 509×1101 on a 390×844 phone), scaled down to at most about 700k px, at least 64 px a side. `crop` and `h` are ignored; `yaw`, `mask`, `rank`, `flags`, `sel`, `pose` and `fx` still apply. The drawing buffer is resized only while drawing; the canvas size, `W`, `Hc`, the scale and the camera are restored in `finally`, in the same task. Returns null if `at` is behind the camera, as well as in the old null cases. The camera's eye is (sin(yaw)·4.6, 1.0, cos(yaw)·4.6) looking at (0, 0.9, 0); framing moves the eye, not the view direction. An invalid `at` or `span` gives the normal shot.

## Weather dares (Phase 6: `web/ui/dares.js`, `wxgate.js`, `darepage.js`, `wxplace.js`, their CSS, and `scripts/check-dares.js`)

### Engine: `web/ui/dares.js`, `dares.css` · prefix `dr` (plus the moved `dare*` names and `rkDareMix`) · classes `.dare`, `.dare-*`

**Purpose**
- One dare a day on Home: a lift for a fading muscle, a life dare, or, from Phase 6, a weather dare that replaces the life dare.
- Moved out of ranks.js in Phase 6. `DARE_LIFE` is now `dareLife()` (ids cold, steps, phone, protein, mob). The Phase 5 seam `rkDareMix()` / `vxOn("dareMix")` is unchanged.

**Start-up rule**
- Function declarations only (67), with state on function properties (`drS.s`, `drWxFx.c`, `uiWireDare.r`).
- Include-time code sits only in 4 try/catch IIFEs: the listeners, and one per water wrapper.
- Loaded after ranks.js and wxgate.js.

**State**
- `S.dares = {scale, tone, won, wxOpt?:"on"|"off", wxWon?, cur}`.
- `cur` kinds:
  - lift `{d, kind:"lift", k, ex, sets, reps, kg, days, st}`
  - life `{d, kind:"life", id, st, sc, at, wxTry?, fx?}`
  - wx `{d, kind:"wx", id:"rain"|"cold"|"wind"|"bright"|"indoor", why, alt, sc, m, h?, st:"new"|"yes"|"no"|"done", at, acc, doneAt, free, seen:{temp, symbol, wind, gust, at}, obj:[{id, n, src, base, done}], wxTry:true, fx?}`
- `S.wx` belongs to native.js. The engine only deletes it, in `drSetWx(false, {forget:true})`.
- Screen state `drS()` = `{soonAt, sig, busy, busyN, wxN, p, pf, pg, q, gen, fgen, sumAt, sumKey, brDur, homeT}`. It is never saved.
  - `busy` ("Checking the forecast…") is true while `busyN` (the checks a tap is waiting on) is above 0.
  - `wxN` counts "wx" emits.
  - `gen` moves on every opt-out (`fgen`: the last one that forgot the place; `pg`: the gen the running check started under). A check that started before an opt-out stops before it reaches the phone again (`drWx` returns `{ok:false, why:"off"}`), and if its silent GPS refresh wrote the place back after a forgetting opt-out, the place is deleted again. So turning weather dares off mid-check never sends the place to MET.

**The weather rules live in wxgate.js (`dw*`, pure).** The engine's only allowed changes, in `drApply(w, now)`, apply only to today's new dare:
1. A life dare with no `wxTry`, while `drWxOn()===true`, gets `dwChoose`. A usable, fresh reading sets `wxTry`; a kind swaps the dare in.
2. An outdoor wx dare on a usable reading that is unsafe or late/night becomes indoor. If the place isn't yours, it goes back to its life dare.
3. A wx dare with `drWxOn()===false` goes back to its life dare. With `null` (caps not here yet) nothing changes; `drView` then draws an unsafe, dark or far new outdoor dare as needing a fresh forecast (below).

**A weather dare belongs to its day.** `drOld(c)`: on a dare from another day, `drAccept` (all kinds), `drRow`, and `drFinish` / `drSkip` (kind wx only) return false. They defer `vxDayCheck()` (behind typeof) and `drHome()`, so the screens move on to today. Life and lift keep the old Done past midnight.

**API**

  - `drRows(id, sc)`.

### Weather gate: `web/ui/wxgate.js` (package gate, prefix `dw`, no CSS)

**Purpose.** It reads one summary from the phone (native/weather.js `summarise()`, MET Norway, CC BY 4.0) and decides today's weather dare:
- `null`: keep the life dare
- `"indoor"`
- an outdoor kind: `rain`, `cold`, `wind` or `bright`

It fails closed on:
- severe weather
- a stale, future or malformed reading, or an unknown symbol
- the wrong place, an unknown phone zone, quiet hours or the dark

**Rules.**
- Every top-level statement is a `function dw*`.
- Tables live on function properties: `dwRules.r` (frozen), `dwSyms.s`, `dwKinds.k`.
- No host globals: no `S`, `NATIVE`, `document`, `window`, `location`, `save`, `setTimeout`, `fetch`, and no `nv*`, `dr*`, `dp*` or `wp*` names.
- No clock: `now` is always an argument and the phone's zone comes in as `c.tz`. Only `dwClock` formats with the host's zone.
- Change a number in `dwRules()` only on purpose, never to make the check pass.

**Context.** `c = {tz, src, placeAt, sumAt, m, quiet}`, built by dares.js `drCtx`. Units: °C, m/s (13.9 = 50 km/h), mm, ms.

**API.**
- `dwRules()` → the numbers
- `dwSyms()` → MET's 41 base codes (a copy)
- `dwSym(s)` → the base code
- `dwKnown(s)` → bool
- `dwNum(...xs)` → bool: every argument is finite
- `dwLL(key)` → `[lat, lon] | null`
- `dwSun(lat, lon, now)` → an exact copy of weather.js `sun()`
- `dwGust(g, wind)` → MET's gust, else wind × 1.6
- `dwHere(lat, lon, now, c)` → `{ok, why: null|"tz"|"zone"|"gps"}`
- `dwLight(lat, lon, now, c, w)` → `{light: "pre"|"day"|"late"|"night", from, until, rise, set, polar}`. The light is judged on the phone's own day:
  - the sun is `dwSun(lat, lon, day0 + 12 h)`, where day0 is local midnight from `c.tz`
  - the reading's sun times count only when they describe that day
  - a polar "night" reading always counts
  - after 21:00 on the phone it is never "pre"
  - under a fixture (`c.quiet===false`) the day is the one around now and there are no quiet hours
- `dwCheck(w, now, key, c)` → `{use, why, age, fresh, recent, safe, here, hereWhy, light, from, until, rise, set, polar, kind, out}`
- `dwChoose(w, now, key, c)` → `{id, why, g}`: the pick rule
- `dwSky(sym)`, `dwWhy(why)`, `dwWarn(why)`: the labels
- `dwTemp(t)` → "8 °C" / "−2 °C" / "–"
- `dwKmh(ms)` → km/h to the nearest 5
- `dwClock(ms)` → "16:42"
- `dwFill(s, {m, h, n})` → fills the placeholders, steps as "1,000"
- `dwCond(w, now, key, c, id, why)` → the conditions line, "" when the reading isn't usable
- `dwKinds()` → `{rain|cold|wind|bright|indoor: {id, name, min, each?, out, lead: {coach, drill}, dark?: {coach, drill}, safety, rows: [{id, t, s, sH?, src, go, n}]}}`, a fresh copy every call. Walk rows have `src:"steps"` and `n:null` (the engine sets n = m × 100). Wind's into/back rows are hand ticks.
- `dwFixtures()` → the 24 preview fixture names
- `dwFixture(name, now)` → `{place, w}`, with exactly the keys of `summarise()`

**State and events.** No state, no events, no wrappers, no DOM. Three files read the gate:
- dares.js, through `drCtx`, `drApply` and `drView`
- darepage.js (`dwClock`)
- wxplace.js (`wpReadLine`: `dwCheck`, `dwRules`, `dwSky`, `dwTemp`)

- **Off**: shown when `drWxState()!=="on"`, or when the place has no coordinates.
  - Contents, top to bottom: glyph `dpGlyph("place")`, headline, body, a bone "Use my location" (`nvLocate(true)`, only ever from this tap), the hint, "or", the town field (`.wp-field` 44 px + Save 44 px), the status line, the privacy line and "Not now".
- **Success**: found and turned on during this visit.
  - Lime "Found … Weather dares are on.", plus "Today's dare is a weather dare now." when `drSetWx` returned `changed`, plus the amber far line when `drHere()` says zone.
  - The main button becomes a bone Done.
- **On**: the place (town, or "Near you") with "Approximate location" or "Typed".
  - The reading line `dwTemp · dwSky · agoTxt(at)` with the MET credit, shown only when `dwCheck(...).use`.
  - Instead of the reading, an amber line when `drHere()` says gps or zone.
  - Then a bone Done, the rows "Use my location" and "Type a different town", and the link "Turn weather dares off".
  - "Type a different town" shows the field. While the field shows, the status line sits under it.

**Keyboard mode:** `.wp-kb` on `#evBody` while the field has focus.
- Everything except `.wp-keep` is hidden. What remains, in order on both pages: the eyebrow, the field, the status and the privacy line.
- Blur clears it 200 ms later.

**Actions:**
- One at a time, as a record `o.wp.act = {k, cut, was}`. `wpAct` starts it and `wpActEnd` ends it. `wpGo(o,a)` (not cut, and this open is still current) is checked after every await.
- **The user's last answer wins:**
  - `wpNotNow` and `wpTurnOff` cut a running action (`wpCut`).
  - A cut action never asks the phone again and never calls `drSetWx(true)`.
  - After a cut by off, `wpCutDone` forgets a place the phone saved meanwhile (`drSetWx(false,{forget:true})`), unless a newer action is running.
  - A page closed mid-action switches nothing on. `wpGone` toasts "Weather dares are on." only if they are on anyway.
- Town lookup: `nvTown` is called only when `NATIVE.on && nvOk()`; any other case shows `wpWhy("browser"|"needs-build")`.
- Both actions first `await nvReady(8000)` while `NATIVE.on && !NATIVE.caps`.
- A place found with coordinates → `await drSetWx(true)`. A name-only place (`saved:"name"`) → the needs-permission line, and dares are not turned on.
- Off → `drSetWx(false,{forget:true})` and a toast.
- Not now → `drSetWx(false,{forget:false})` on a first answer, and also when it cut an action while wxOpt is "on"; then the page closes.
- When `TAB==="you"`, `renderYou()` runs afterwards.

**State:** screen state only, on the open's `o.wp`: `{mode, busy, act, msg, news, field, q, dirty, blurT}`. It is never saved.
- wxplace.js writes neither `S.wx` (native.js does) nor `S.dares` (the engine does).
- Copy for errors: `wpWhy(why)`: browser, needs-build, denied, off, timeout, not-found, offline, empty, needs-permission, else "Something went wrong. Try again."

**Events:**
- Listens to `vxOn("dare")` for `what` "opt" or "wx".
- Deferred redraw through `evRefresh()`, only while the view is "wxplace".
- Skipped while an `evSheet` is open (sets `evS().dirty`), while an action is busy, and while the field has focus (sets `o.wp.dirty` and redraws on blur).
- Never emits, never fetches, never saves.

**Settings row:**

- The Weather dares row is always there. The Phone check row shows only while `nvCheck.on`. Both go before `.ph-wg`.
- Row `#phWx` opens `wpOpen({from:"settings"})`. There is no temperature, so no credit. Its sub and value:

**native.js:**
- `nvReady(ms=8000)` → Promise<bool>. It resolves at once in the browser or when caps exist; otherwise on the first caps message (then unsubscribes), or `!!NATIVE.caps` after ms.
- `nvLocate`: `caps.loc` is set to "denied" only when `canAsk===false`, and to "undetermined" when `canAsk===true`.

**trophy.js:**
- Weatherproof: `cnt("weatherproof", …, [3,10,25], …, S.dares.wxWon, "rain")`, placed after Grit Master. Training now has 11; the room holds 43 trophies / 93 grades.
- `troLine(id)` → `{name, g, n, gname, prog, done}` or null. It is pure and never calls `troState()`. g is the higher of the grade earned and the grade already met.
