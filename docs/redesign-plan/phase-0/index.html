Ossa 2.0 · Phase 0 of 7

The look

The new palette, Inter, glass and hexagons run through the whole app. The six screens that already existed now look like the designs. Nothing works differently. It's all on the branch redesign-v2, not committed and not sent anywhere.

6 screens restyled 1,331 old colours remapped 713 now read from tokens fonts 191 → 134 KB 0 native changes

What changed

  • Pure black, charcoal cards, neon lime #D4FF00, and the brief's rank colours, on screens and on the 3D body.
  • Inter everywhere, mostly 800–900, with italic 900 for big moments like the rank-up.
  • Glass panels over the body, hexagon rank badges, a filled tab bar with a dot on the tab you're on.
  • The 3D body reads its rank colours from the design tokens, and a picked muscle gets a lime edge, so it never looks like a white Strong muscle.
  • Workout steppers now show the number they change.
  • Under the hood, the 3D engine is ready for Phase 1's red sore spots. A broken effect can't drop you to the flat body any more, and the body rebuilds itself if iOS drops it.

Not checked yet

  • Your iPhone. Glass smoothness, Inter and the black edges need a look in Expo Go (steps below). The desktop preview isn't the iPhone's WebView.
  • An independent review found 15 real problems. All 15 are fixed (list below).
  • The Mind and Breathe screens keep their violets until Phase 3, when Breathe becomes Focus.
  • I haven't committed anything. The baseline commit and backup were made before I started.

Check it on your iPhone

Expo Go · no build needed

Steps

  1. On the PC, in the project folder: npm start
  2. On the iPhone, open Expo Go and scan the QR code (same Wi-Fi).
  3. Body page: does the black meet the phone's edges with no grey band? Are the legend and 3D button readable?
  4. Start a workout and log two sets: does the body behind stay smooth, and do the steppers update the number?
  5. Open Muscle ranks and the streak (tap the flame chip).

What would be a problem

  • Stutter while the body moves behind the workout (too much glass blur).
  • Text that looks thin or blurry (Inter renders differently on iPhone).
  • A grey band at the top or bottom, or a white flash on launch. The launch screen itself is a Build 9 item.
  • A flat 2D body instead of the 3D one, which would mean a shader problem.

Decide before Phase 1

My pick first
Bench and the front delts

The plan page used 50% for front delts on bench. The reviewers settled on 20%: at 35–50% the front delts rank up mostly from pressing and overtake the side delts. So the bench example becomes Chest +3, Triceps +1, Front delts +1 (not +2).

My pick20%, as in the table.

The muscle a session picked the exercise for

With one main muscle per exercise, some plan slots would stop training the muscle they were picked for (Band pull-apart for rhomboids, Inverted row for lats, Dips for pec minor, Single-leg RDL for side glutes and others).

My pickThe muscle the session picked it for always gets 100%, as today. The helpers in this table apply to the rest.

A cap on helper credit

Helper credit adds up within a session, so two small helpers can earn +1 every session forever. Glutes are the fastest muscle everywhere (about 8 weeks to Unbreakable), with traps next.

My pickCap each muscle's helper credit at half a 3-set primary per session.

Stretches and posture drills

The Doorway pec stretch is pec minor's third exercise, so it would earn full training credit. So would Chin tucks, Cat-cow and the foam-roller extension.

My pickStretches and posture drills earn no rank points. They still count for recovery and the streak.

Pec minor, shins, soleus, lower back

No regular day trains pec minor, and the ranks screen calls it 'Upper chest', which no incline press credits. Shins, soleus and lower back aren't in any split, so shins stay Unranked for anyone on a programme.

My pickRename pec minor to its own name, and add one of these to an existing day each week. I'll propose the days in Phase 1.

A few main muscles a coach could argue with

Rack pull counts as traps (not lower back), Walking lunge as glutes (not quads), Sumo squat as adductors, Prone Y-raise as traps (side delts 60%).

My pickKeep as in the table.

What 'kit' means

Backpack curl and Farmer's carry are tagged dumbbell but are the bodyweight options. Pull-up, Dips and Dead hang are tagged gym but the code treats them as bodyweight.

My pick'Kit' means what you need to own: a bar to hang from counts as bodyweight. I'll retag before anything filters on it.
