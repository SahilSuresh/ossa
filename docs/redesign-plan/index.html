What the code told me

8 readers + 1 critic, read-only

Good news

  • Six screens already match the designs' layout and wording. For those, Phase 0 is colour, type and shape only.
  • The body already has a 13-bone rig with exercise poses (one sits on a chair). A seated Monk Mode pose is realistic. The brief thought there was no pose system.
  • Notifications, the share sheet and resting heart rate already go through the app's bridge. The nudge, Monk timer, heartbeat and card need no new native code.
  • Overall rank, best set and est. 1-rep max already exist, as on the rank-up design.

Watch out

  • Fading is switched off in rank colours, so the Body page never shows it today.
  • Helper muscles get a flat 33% of RP. 17 exercises give them nothing at all.
  • Live bug: after 90 sessions the 100-session milestone and the streak screen stop.
  • A save that doesn't match is silently reset to zero, and there's no backup. 2.0 adds about a dozen saved fields.
  • One shader error on an iPhone drops everyone to the flat 2D body.
  • Open-Meteo's free tier is non-commercial, and Ossa sells Pro. MET Norway is free with a credit.
  • No over-the-air updates: every change, web or native, reaches TestFlight only in a new build. Expo Go is for testing.
  • The camera permission promises a fingertip pulse reading that nothing uses (App Review risk). web/ isn't in git.

Where the readers disagreed, I picked

  • Restyle each existing screen once, in Phase 0. Later phases only add their data to it.
  • One owner file for the engine (RP, fading, recovery). Other phases plug in through hooks, so no two files override the same function.
  • The aura is drawn by the body's own renderer, not a second 3D canvas. That saves battery and memory.
  • One shape for Sore spots data, and left/right checked on screen before any UI is built on it.
  • All native work goes into one build (9), so later phases need none.

Phase 1

Vessel Engine

Rank points that follow the kinetic chain, fading you can see on your body, and Sore spots that glow red and pause the fade. Your saved data survives the upgrade.

No native buildFixes 6 known bugs

What changes

  • web/ui/vessel.js
    Save safety: a version stamp, a one-time copy of your pre-2.0 data on the phone, and one migration step at start-up.
  • web/ui/vessel.js
    The engine owner: one RP function used by Workout complete, quick log and the session chips. Hooks for later phases (after a workout, reminders, notification taps).
  • web/ossa.html · SEC
    Helper weights for all 83 exercises, from your marked-up table. Bench: chest 100%, front delts 50%, triceps 35%.

    Fading shows on the Body page as a dimmed rank colour. One neglect rule (today 7, 10 and 21 days disagree).
  • web/ui/injury.jsinjury.css
    Sore spots: flag a muscle or joint, left, right or both. It glows red, its fade pauses, plan rows say "You flagged your left knee", and Relief routine opens the joint routine. The physio/GP line stays.
  • ranks.js
    Muscle ranks shows our 3D body in rank colours instead of the 2D drawing. Rank-ups show the muscle you trained first.
  • docs/BUGS.md
    Bugs in the same code: 01, 02, 03, 05, 08, 10. A better demo seed for every later screenshot.
  • preview switch
    Two spikes, not shipped: the seated Monk pose and the Tank/Scout shape change. Their screenshots decide Phases 3 and 5.

What you'll see

  • Workout complete after 4×8 bench: Chest +3, Front delts +2, Triceps +1 RP. Today it gives +3, +1, +1.
  • The Body page with quads untrained for 15 days, then the same with your left knee flagged (fade paused).
  • Sore spots next to the design, plus a 10-second video of the red glow.
  • A left-knee test from front and back, proving the side is right.
  • Spike screenshots: lotus or block sit, and Tank, neutral and Scout.

Phase 4

Recover

Fuel you log in grams and meals, and a reminder after training to do a 3-minute reset for the muscles you just worked.

No native buildReal notification in Expo Go

What changes

  • web/ui/recover.jsrecover.css
    Fuel on the Recover tab: protein in grams (+15, +25, +40), carbs as meals (2 of 3). The target is 1.6 g per kg if you add your weight. It's optional, never asked under 18, and counted in portions otherwise.
  • web/ui/recover.js
    Game rule: hitting protein and carbs makes "ready in" up to 8 hours sooner a day, never more than a quarter of the wait. Labelled "Game rule".
  • web/ui/recover.js
    A Fuel screen on our 3D body with the recovering muscle lit.
  • web/ui/recover.jsworkout.js
    Recovery nudge: a local notification 45 minutes after a workout ("3 minutes for your chest"). Tapping it opens a 3-move reset. "Remind me in 15 min" is there too.

What you'll see

  • The Fuel card before and after logging, and the Fuel screen next to the design.
  • A table of ready times with and without fuel.
  • On your phone with a 1-minute test delay: the notification arrives, tap, the reset opens.

RiskNutrition is health-adjacent. Every line stays "logged" or "game rule", with a GP or dietitian line.

Phase 6

Challenge

On days with notable, safe weather, the daily life dare becomes a weather dare. It's still one dare a day, and saying no still costs nothing.

Uses Build 9's location

What changes

  • web/ui/dares.jsdares.css
    Dares move out of ranks.js into their own file. Weather dares are opt-in, using approximate location or a town you type. Readings are cached for an hour and credited.
  • web/ui/dares.js
    Safety gate: never in thunder, ice, gusts of 50 km/h or more, below 0 °C, above 30 °C or after dark. Those days get an indoor dare.
  • scripts/check-dares.js
    A check that runs every severe weather case and fails if any gives an outdoor dare.
  • web/ui/dares.js
    Objectives tick themselves where the app knows: steps from Apple Health, water from the water log.

What you'll see

  • A rain dare, the indoor fallback, the "type your town" state and the Home card, next to the design.
  • The safety check's output: every severe case gives an indoor dare.
  • A recording from your phone: allow approximate location, the town appears, Accept, tick, Done.
