# SUMMARY
Build "First steps": the body guides you itself in its own speech bubble. The bubble shows a five-stone log, and stone 1 is already broken by the intro. Four real taps (your weak link, Train, the quest, Start) take you into a short first quest aimed at your weak link, about 15 minutes instead of 40. It is sized so the first session really earns your first rank and Level 1. That first rank cracks the stone on the body. The last stone breaks when you come back the next day and check your recovery, and the body says "I'm awake now." It is mostly Design 3, with Design 1's quest log, no-scrim glow and finale, Design 2's one look at the endgame (no new shader), and all of Judge 3's code fixes.

# FINISH
There are two moments, both from the app's own world and neither paying out tutorial RP. First, the run after the first workout gets a 'First rank' beat. On the body, the weak muscle cracks (stone chips, lime pulse, knock and haptic). Then the hex badge arrives as an uncut stone that cracks and shatters to show 'Fragile I · {Traps} woke up', with confetti and chime. Level 1 follows with 'Ranks can fade. Your level never drops.' The user is then brought back to the Body page: a half-turn, every newly ranked muscle ripples lime, stone 4 breaks and the body says 'Lit up. See you {Wednesday}.' Second, the next day the Recover seal invites a check-in. Tapping the recovery ring breaks stone 5, a lime wave runs across all five tab icons, and the body says 'I'm awake now. Each session breaks one more open.', the intro's own line coming true. That unlocks nothing new (it's a hard paywall, everything is already open). It marks the core loop as learned: train, rank up, level, recover. From then on the Profile row reads 'First steps · Done', and the lime ring has already moved to tomorrow's weak link.
